## 🔍 How Pricing Data Works

This tool pulls data from Amazon Web Services to ensure the estimates stay relevant. It looks at standard instance types and region costs. You can select your preferred region from the menu inside the app to see how geographic location impacts your total bill. The app does not store your AWS account credentials or sensitive private keys. It only performs calculations based on the settings you input.

## 📄 Managing Your Projects

The application allows you to save your work. When you configure a complex setup, use the "Save Project" button in the file menu. The app creates a small file on your computer. You can open this file later to adjust your numbers or share it with team members. These files contain no personal data and only track the specific instance configurations you chose during your session.

## 💡 Best Practices for Estimating

Use these tips to get the most accurate results:

*   Define your storage needs early. Disk space costs vary significantly by region.
*   Account for data transfer fees if your project requires high bandwidth.
*   Check the "Region" setting before you finalize your estimate, as prices change based on where you host your data.
*   Update the application regularly to ensure you have the latest pricing information from the provider.

## 🛡️ Security and Privacy

Your privacy remains a priority. This application does not connect to your personal AWS environment. It acts as a local calculator. It does not send your data to third-party servers. All calculations happen on your machine. You control all input data, and you decide where to save your project files.
